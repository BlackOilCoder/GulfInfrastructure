Data notes

Bathymetry coverage

BOEM’s deepwater grid covers over 90,000 sq mi. Depths are in feet; native cells are 40 × 40 ft. The hillshade uses 5× vertical exaggeration. The source grid does not cover the entire Gulf.

Depth contours

BOEM contours from the same seismic grid, labeled in feet and stored as local tiles. The interval follows map scale: 1,000 ft zoomed out, 500 ft at regional scale, 100 ft zoomed in (heavier, labeled index lines every 5,000 / 2,500 / 500 ft; every 100 ft line is labeled closer than 1:100,000).

Ocean currents

Copernicus Marine Service Global Ocean Physics Analysis and Forecast (GLOBAL_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_001_024, 1/12° ≈ 9 km, DOI 10.48670/moi-00016). Modelled values for situational awareness only, not for operational or design use. Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information.

Platform details

Structure names, business associates, coordinates, water depth and related records are joined to map points by Complex ID. This local BSEE dataset snapshot is dated October 19, 2026.

Infrastructure detail

BSEE’s pipeline service provides oil & gas pipeline segments. The source schema does not identify a separate umbilical layer; umbilical coverage may therefore be incomplete. GIS locations are approximate, not official records.

Talos Active Assets

Matches workbook platform Complex IDs, pipeline Segment Numbers and well API numbers to BSEE map features. Turning this layer off also switches off the three full BSEE infrastructure layers; reenable any of them to show the broader BSEE data.
